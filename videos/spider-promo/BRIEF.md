## Intent

סרטון תדמית (פרומו) לאתר SPIDER — https://devopsdevopshaim-wq.github.io/devops-hub/portfolio/ —
של חיים קריספין: "כל מה שבניתי, במקום אחד". המשתמש ביקש "שתפתיע אותי ברמה ממש מקצועית".

Concept (picked by the gate, user said "surprise me"): **"הרשת"** — the video is a spider web being spun.
It opens in darkness on one silk thread dropping from the top of frame; every project is a node the thread
reaches, lights up and shows its real screenshot; the strands keep connecting fields (AI agents, websites,
engineering planners, finance, Torah) until the whole web is visible and resolves into the SPIDER logo.
The thesis: one builder, many connected systems. Motion rides on drawn SVG strands (stroke draw-on),
a camera that travels along the threads between nodes, and a count-up to 48.

## Customizations

- Count-up to the number of projects (48).
- Real captured screenshots of the projects (portfolio/shots/*.jpg) as the proof, not mockups.
- Hebrew, RTL kinetic typography; works with sound off (feeds autoplay muted).
