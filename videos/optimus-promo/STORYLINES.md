# Storylines (picked: A)

**A. "One night, a hundred magnets"** (picked): a moment in someone's day, as an object-to-object chain. 21:40 at a wedding, the guests' photos keep arriving on the phone, they burst into a pile, the Optimus shutter closes and opens on the studio: one fix for every photo, a frame from the couple's invitation (16 AI designs), AI on the real guests, the print, the magnet on the fridge, the end card.

**B. "From invitation to magnet"**: the result first. A finished magnet on a fridge; the camera pulls back through how it was made: the invitation, the 16 AI frames, the editor, the print. Light, ivory and gold.

**C. "The aperture"** (bold): match cuts on the Optimus aperture. It opens on each world (editing, frames, AI, print), giant kinetic type cut on the beat between them. Black and gold.
